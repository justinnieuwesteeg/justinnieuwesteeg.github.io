Claim Your Listing

Take Control

Are you a local business owner? We want to make sure your information is always accurate and up-to-date. We offer local business owners the opportunity to "claim" their listing on South Padre Live.

Claiming your listing allows you to:

  • Establish a relationship to maintain up-to-date and accurate information in real time.
  • Ensure your location, hours, and website are correct.
  • Be eligible for future promotional opportunities, like "Featured Listings."

To get started, please send us an email at southpadrespecials@gmail.com with the subject line "Claim My Listing" and let us know your business name. We'll get in touch to verify ownership and set you up.

Accuracy of listed offers not guaranteed. Please contact the establishment to confirm validity of the offer.
