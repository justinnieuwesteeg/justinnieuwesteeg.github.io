Claim Your Listing

Are you a local business owner? We want to make sure your information is always accurate and up-to-date. We offer local business owners the opportunity to "claim" their listing on South Padre Live.

Claiming your listing allows you to:

  • Maintain up-to-date information in real time.
  • Ensure your location, hours, and website are correct.
  • Be eligible for "Featured Listings."

To get started, please message us on Facebook or send us an email at southpadrespecials@gmail.com with the subject line "Claim My Listing" and let us know your business name.

Accuracy of listed offers not guaranteed. Please contact the establishment to confirm validity of the offer.
